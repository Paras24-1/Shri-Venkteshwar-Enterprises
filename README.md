## 🌟 Overview & Key Offerings

Shri Venkteshwar Enterprises (SVE) provides bespoke corporate, business, and retail financing across India, operating with a **strict "Zero Advance Fees" policy** (processing fees are payable strictly **after disbursement**).

### Featured Products on Landing Page
1. **Private Business Funding**: Short-term (ROI 2%–5%/mo, 15-day EMI cheque-based) & Long-term (~1%/mo against property/BG).
   - Regional Turnover Criteria:
     - **Punjab & Chandigarh:** ₹1 Cr+
     - **Delhi & NCR:** ₹5 Cr+
     - **Haryana & Himachal Pradesh:** ₹50 Cr+
     - **Bengaluru, Chennai, Mumbai, Pune:** ₹50 Cr+
     - **Rest of India:** ₹100 Cr+
2. **Large Corporate Funding**: ₹10 Cr+ short-term (1.5%–3%/mo) & long-term (7%–12% p.a.).
3. **Startup & Project Funding (DPR)**: ₹25 Cr to ₹500 Cr+ based on Detailed Project Report.
4. **NPA Account Solutions**: Secured funding against collateral for distressed businesses.
5. **Bank & NBFC Loans**: Personal Loans (Min ₹1L, Salary ₹15k+), Business Loans (₹25L+, CIBIL 725+), Loan Against Property (LAP, 30-year property title chain).
6. **Defence & Civil/Govt Personnel Loans**: Exclusive schemes for Army, Navy, Air Force, BSF, CRPF, State/Central Govt, and Punjab Government employees (available in Punjabi & English).
7. **Small Business / Startup Loans**: ₹3 Lakh – ₹10 Lakh (24% p.a., 7% PF, no business vintage needed).
8. **Emergency Cash Funding**: Daily/Weekly/Monthly cash flow funding.
9. **Partnership Network**: DSAs, Financial Consultants, and Referral Partners (1% commission / per-file payout) & Institutional Investors/Financiers.

## 🛠️ How to Deploy

### Option 1: GitHub Pages (Recommended - 1 Click)
1. Go to repository **Settings** in GitHub.
2. Under **Code and automation**, click **Pages**.
3. Under **Branch**, select `main` and root `/`.
4. Click **Save**. Your site will be live at `https://paras24-1.github.io/Shri-Venkteshwar-Enterprises/` within 60 seconds!

### Option 2: Vercel / Netlify
- Simply import this GitHub repository. Since it is a static web app (`index.html`), it deploys with zero build configuration!
