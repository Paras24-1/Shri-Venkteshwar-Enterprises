Do you charge any advance fee or upfront documentation charges?

No, absolutely not. SVE maintains a strict policy: we never charge any upfront processing or file charges before loan approval. Any fees are strictly settled only after disbursement.

How fast can private business funding be disbursed?

Once complete documents (bank statement, GST returns, and video verification) are submitted, preliminary sanction and terms are established within 24 to 48 hours, followed by disbursement upon cheque exchange and formal agreement.

Yes! For NPA accounts, we arrange secured funding against adequate property collateral (residential, commercial, or industrial) with clear titles to help businesses normalize their cash flow.
